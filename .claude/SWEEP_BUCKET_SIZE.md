**Default (`bucket_size=None` -> auto = ceil(log2 N))** keeps the historic
behaviour: minimum bits/key, slowest build. Right when space is the
binding constraint or N is small.

**`bucket_size=8`** is the build-speed default to recommend to consumers
that build many PHFs (cipher-maps' construction loop builds one PHF per
construction; if a workflow rebuilds frequently or at scale, this is the
spot to be). At 100K keys it cuts build time by ~6x for ~2x bits/key.

**Below `bucket_size=4`** the bits/key cost rises faster than the
build savings: `bs=2` is 8 bpk at every N, only marginally faster than
`bs=4` (~4 bpk). Skip.

**Above auto** (i.e. above `ceil(log2 N)`) consistently fails to build at
the chosen retry budget and is not exposed in this sweep.
